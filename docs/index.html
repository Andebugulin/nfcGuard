nfcGuard

Apps you can't open

Lock distracting apps behind a physical NFC tag. Keep the tag somewhere inconvenient, like the kitchen or a gym bag, and opening Instagram costs a walk instead of a thumb.

How It Works
FOUR STEPS
TO FOCUS
01

Create Modes

Pick the apps to block, or allow only the essential ones. One mode for work, one for sleep, one for study.

02

Link NFC Tags

Register tags as unlock keys. Each one decides how much it gives you: full access, or five minutes before the mode snaps back on.

03

Set Schedules

Per-day schedules turn modes on by themselves. Different hours for weekdays and weekends.

04

Stay Focused

A blocked app is covered or closed outright. To get in, walk to the tag and tap it. The friction is the feature.
